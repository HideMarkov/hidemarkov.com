About us

Enterprise experience. SME speed.

FOUNDER

Sebastian Kustosz

I founded HideMarkov because clarity about your own processes is an opportunity to grow. Even large corporations keep finding gaps there. An SME that knows its processes can seize that opportunity.

Since 2019, as an IT auditor, I have examined the processes of banks, insurers and airlines, with a focus on expected credit loss (ECL) calculations at banks, key controls at insurers and highly automated revenue processes at airlines. The questions were always the same: how do processes really run, where do errors arise, and how do automated controls mitigate the risks?

My foundation: an apprenticeship at Bayer Industry Services in Leverkusen, a degree in business chemistry from Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf and two peer-reviewed papers in organic synthesis (ChemCatChem, as first author; European Journal of Organic Chemistry). Hence my approach: measure first, then change.

What this means for your business: our software reconstructs one of your processes from your system data. You see where time is lost and what can be automated, and you decide what gets implemented. Kaliber, our calculation software for scaffolding companies, shows what this looks like in practice.

One point of contact from analysis to finished solution, and a fixed price before work begins. I speak German, English and Polish.

FAQ

What you want to know before the first call

Short answers on cost, data and process. Everything else we settle in the intro call.

What does a Process Mining Check cost?

A fixed price that we agree after the intro call, before any work starts. The intro call itself is non-binding.

Which data do you need?

An export from the system where the process is recorded, for example ERP, CRM, a ticket system or Excel. We tell you exactly which fields we need.

What happens to our data?

We sign a confidentiality agreement and a data processing agreement. Your data is analysed on a computer in Switzerland and deleted when the work is done.

Which companies is it for?

For SMEs with processes that repeat often and are recorded in a system, for example quotes, orders, work reports and invoices in trades, construction, retail or services.

Do you replace our existing software?

No. We look at where work between your programs is still done by hand and close exactly those gaps.

Do you come on site?

Yes. In the Schaffhausen region we are happy to come to you for the intro call; otherwise we talk by video.
